# ROAST MAP: The Real Compass to Target Balance Note
### Long Trip Trouble vs Fast Trip Problem

**By: Supriyanto**
**Cengkudu Coffee Roastary**

## Roasting is not about heat. Roasting is a journey.

A bean is like a human. It has a natural phase that must be passed in order, it cannot be skipped. There are only four conditions: right fire, right temperature, bean dried to the maximum, and the best selected beans.

Moisture content must be right. The bean must be dry to the maximum. If the bean is still wet, the journey cannot start. The wet grass smell will stay too long.

And after moisture, there is one more that is the most important:

**The best selected beans, no defects.**

Beans must be selected. Defective, broken, black, holed beans, throw them away. Don't bring them on the journey. Because if from the start the bean is already defective, no matter how good the Map is, no matter how correct the Compass is, the final taste will definitely be defective too.

If these four are right, the taste will definitely be balanced.

That journey needs two things: Map and Compass.

The Map shows where you are. The Compass shows if your direction is correct to the target.

Without Map and Compass, the roaster will definitely get lost. And getting lost has two kinds:

### 1. LONG TRIP TROUBLE - Too Long on the Road
Unstable fire, gas runs out, roaster gets stuck in drying. Wet grass smell sticks too long. Caramel is not formed. The result in cupping: aor taste, like raw eggplant, like raw peanut.

### 2. FAST TRIP PROBLEM - Too Fast to Arrive
Strong wind, temperature jumps from 190°C to 220°C. Roaster speeds up, skips the caramel phase. First crack explodes inside the drum. Smoke is trapped, sticks to the bean. The result: smoky, ash smell, dark roast. Only burnt bitter, no sweetness.

## THE SOLUTION: DROP JUST BEFORE FIRST CRACK, LET FIRST CRACK HAPPEN OUTSIDE.

Don't wait for first crack inside. Drop when it is just about to crack. Let it crack outside, in open air. The smoke is released, it does not stick to the bean.

That is how your Compass keeps pointing to the target.

## OUR TARGET BALANCE NOTE

In cupping, it must be clean: caramel, sweetness, bold, balance, bitter at the end but long taste sweetness. A taste that is ngangenin, makes you miss it, makes you want it again. No smoky, no aor.

That is Roast Map. It is not a timer. It does not take over the roaster.

It is The Real Compass. A Map that accompanies the roaster on the journey. Eyes see the temperature, nose smells the aroma, ears hear the first crack. All three walk together, pointing to one point: Target Balance Note.

Roast Map does not replace the roaster. It keeps the roaster from getting lost, from Long Trip Trouble, from Fast Trip Problem, so the target note is always achieved.
